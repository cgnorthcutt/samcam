The opportunity

A data flywheel rooted in the work itself.

The physical AI opportunity may ultimately be measured in hundreds of billions - perhaps trillions - of dollars. But the credible path begins much smaller: one design partner, one valuable use case, and one form factor people will actually wear.

Samsara's potential advantage is not generic scale. It is the ability to serve blue-collar teams on site, build trust over time, and collect fully egocentric data while people perform real work with dignity. That creates a dataset most labs cannot reproduce by hiring gig workers to stage isolated tasks.
